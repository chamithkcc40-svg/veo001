## 5. Mix targets (labelled DEFAULTS)
- Integrated loudness: about **-14 LUFS** for YouTube (DEFAULT for long-form), true peak at or below -1 dBTP.
- Voice: the anchor. Peaks around -6 to -3 dBFS, kept consistent between Part 1 and Part 2 (normalize both files to the same loudness before editing).
- Music bed under voice: about -20 to -24 dB relative to the voice, with **ducking of 6-10 dB** while the voice plays (sidechain, attack about 50 ms, release about 400 ms), and swells up to full in voice gaps and at the peak.
- SFX: -12 to -18 dB under the voice. Siren pass-bys can momentarily reach -8 dB in the cold open only, and only where no voice line sits on top.
- Ambience: -28 to -34 dB (see table).
- Voice-forward rule: no SFX or music transient may land on a key word ("drops", "squeezed", "stretched", "redshift", "expanding"). Nudge SFX by up to 4 frames to avoid it.
- Siren pitch and the visuals must match: the rise and drop in the siren audio has to land on the frame where the ambulance passes Pip.
