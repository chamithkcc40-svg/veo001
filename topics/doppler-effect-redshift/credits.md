# Credits - The Doppler Effect (doppler-effect-redshift)

Final duration: **193.0 s** (5790 frames at 30 fps, 1920x1080). Target in handoff.md: 180 s.
The voiceover (185.68 s) sets the length; nothing was sped up or cut.

## Artwork
Every character, prop, environment, chart and effect is drawn in code (SVG/CSS in Remotion).
No company logos are used (the script names no companies), so no logo files and no simple-icons.
Historical figures (Doppler, Buys Ballot, Slipher, Hubble, Lemaitre) are original, respectful
period-costume cartoon designs; they never speak.

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1, embedded as local woff2 in remotion/public/fonts)
- Nunito (captions, body) - Vernon Adams, Cyreal, Jacques Le Bailly.
- Fredoka (display, labels) - Milena Brandao, Hafontia.
- IM Fell DW Pica (1842 period documents only) - Igino Marini.

## Audio
- Voiceover: supplied by the producer (resources/voiceover/part_01.mp3, part_02.mp3).
- SFX: 17 files from the producer's library (resources/SFX: whooshes, pops, clicks, riser, stretch).
- Siren, horns, pond, chalk, steam, ambience beds and the score: synthesized in Python
  (numpy/scipy, seeded) by remotion/scripts/audio/. No third-party recordings.

## Data
Hubble diagram is schematic (24 galaxies after Hubble 1929, PNAS 2015 summary); colours exaggerated, as labelled on screen.
